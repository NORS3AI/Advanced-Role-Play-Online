# Advanced Role Play Online (ARPO)

**An online roleplaying application built for players who want an easy way to
configure and explain their characters.** Built for **World of Warcraft**, and
designed to expand to other games, stories, and worlds.

---

## What is ARPO?

ARPO gives roleplayers a simple, structured way to build, store, and share
their characters online. Instead of scattering notes across documents and chat
logs, players get one place to define who their character is, how they look,
and the story that drives them — then share that profile with the people they
play with.

The first target is **World of Warcraft** roleplay, but the character model is
built to be generic so it can be extended to other games, tabletop settings,
and original worlds.

## Goals

- **Easy configuration** — a guided setup so anyone can describe a character
  without wrestling with a complicated interface.
- **Rich character profiles** — appearance, personality, background, and
  relationships in one shareable place.
- **Shareable** — send other players a link to your character.
- **Game-agnostic core** — start with WoW, expand to other games, stories, and
  worlds.

## Status

🚧 **Early development.** ARPO is in its initial setup phase. The features above
describe the project's direction and are not all implemented yet.
